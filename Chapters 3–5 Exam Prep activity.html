This checklist covers happiness and subjective wellbeing (Chapter 3), eudaimonic wellbeing (Chapter 4), and optimism, explanatory style and hope (Chapter 5). For each concept, tick the box when you have reviewed it. Then rate how well you know it from 1 to 5. Be honest: the summary at the end turns your ratings into a study plan.

How to rate yourself

1I don't know this yet
2I recognise the term
3I can define it
4I can explain it with a study or example
5I can apply it to a case and compare it with related ideas

Aim for 4 or 5 on every concept before the exam. Essay questions need level 5.
